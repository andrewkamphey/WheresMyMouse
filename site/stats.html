Source is the link's utm_source if it had one, otherwise the referring site, otherwise the in-app browser (TikTok and Instagram often hide the referrer), otherwise direct. Each visitor counts once a day, under the source of their first visit that day, and a download from a Download button is credited to that visit. Downloads from a link straight to /download (like the GitHub README) count without a visit, so a download rate can pass 100%. iPads count as non-Mac. Only daily totals are stored: no cookies, no IP addresses, no full referrer URLs.
