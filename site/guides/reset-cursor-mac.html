How to Get Your Mac Cursor Back to Normal

Updated 2026-10-23 · macOS 12 Monterey to macOS 26 Tahoe

Quick answer: Open System Settings → Accessibility → Display → Pointer. Drag Pointer size all the way left to Normal, then click Reset Colors to get the white outline and black fill back.

  1. Open System Settings

    Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner of the screen and choose System Settings.

  2. Go to Accessibility, then Display

    Click Accessibility in the sidebar (you may need to scroll down), then click Display.

  3. Scroll down to Pointer

    Scroll to the Pointer section. Everything about the pointer is here: Shake mouse pointer to locate, Pointer size, Pointer outline color, Pointer fill color and Reset Colors.

  4. Drag Pointer size all the way left

    Far left is the normal size.

  5. Click Reset Colors

    This puts back the white outline and black fill.

If the cursor grows for a second when you move it

That's Shake mouse pointer to locate, which is on by default. Turn it off in the same section if it bothers you. See shake to find your cursor.

If the cursor has disappeared

  • Shake the mouse or move your finger quickly on the trackpad: the pointer grows so you can spot it.
  • If you have more than one display, it may be on the other screen. Keep moving it toward the edge you think it went past.
  • Move the pointer onto the Dock, or press Command-Tab to switch apps. Some people report this brings a vanished pointer back.

If you use Where's My Mouse?, choose Default in its menu to go back to the normal pointer in one click. Your recording and presenting looks stay saved as presets for next time.

Questions

How do I get my cursor back to normal on a Mac?

Open System Settings → Accessibility → Display → Pointer, drag Pointer size to the far left, and click Reset Colors.

How do I make my cursor smaller on a Mac?

Drag the Pointer size slider to the left in Accessibility → Display.

Why is my cursor so big on my Mac?

Either the Pointer size slider was moved, or Shake mouse pointer to locate is enlarging it while you move the mouse quickly.
